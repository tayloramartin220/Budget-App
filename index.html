Categories & groups

Group related categories together (e.g. Takeout + Coffee → Eating Out). The "Where your money went" chart rolls each group up into one line you can tap to expand. Move a category with its Group dropdown; rename a group by clicking its name. Tick "Savings / investing" on a group to keep money you're putting away out of your spending total.

Exchange rates

Used to convert your Net Worth, Investments, and Savings Goals into the currency you pick on the Dashboard ("Show in:"). This tool works offline and never fetches live rates — update these by hand whenever you want fresher numbers.

Both rates are in ISK — e.g. "121.8" means 1 USD = 121.8 kr.

Assets things you own that aren't cash or investments

A house, a car, anything else with real value. Enter its current market value (not what you paid for it) — this adds to your net worth. If it carries a debt (like a mortgage or car loan), add that debt on the Debts tab and link it here to see your equity; your net worth still counts the full asset value and the full debt separately, so nothing gets double-counted.
